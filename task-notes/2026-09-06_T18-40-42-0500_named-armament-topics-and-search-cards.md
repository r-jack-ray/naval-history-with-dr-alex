# Named armament topics and search cards

Updated 2026-09-06 19:53:23 America/Chicago.

## Intent and use in future improvements

Help readers identify a directly named weapon from its topic title and a short spot-reference descriptor. Correct official designation punctuation and acronym casing without turning generic calibre groups into speculative model records. Use curated summaries in topic search cards so related-video lists do not appear as unexplained excerpts. Preserve useful registry metadata even when no authored video currently references the topic.

Use this note as the continuing decision record. Update the change tables, sources, exceptions and validation status whenever a later pass changes these topics. Verify live registry and policy state before applying an old recommendation.

## Evidence and maintenance method

- Authored video and segment references establish which weapon a topic means. Official service, museum, government, manufacturer and research sources establish designation spelling, basic role and useful distinguishing details. A factual topic descriptor does not revise or strengthen a speaker’s claims in a video segment.
- Each summary table row retains the supporting source URLs. Recheck the linked sources when changing a descriptor, especially for development status, model identity, variant roles, operators or historical naming systems.
- Keep titles as names. Keep summaries compact and free of repeated names or opening articles. Include family, variant or project qualifiers when omitting them would imply capabilities or service status that are not established.
- Add exact display rules for reviewed designators and update matching creation rules. Avoid a universal letter-number rewrite across weapons, aircraft, vessels and historical national naming systems.
- Deduplicate aliases only after comparing the site’s normalized search keys; retain meaningful alternate spellings, acronyms, accents and joined forms. Update policy aliases as well as registry aliases.
- Retain unused records and their curated metadata. An unused count is informational or a warning, not a deletion queue.
- Preserve the distinction between an unresolved possible merge and a verified title correction. Molins installation scope remains a reason to retain separate records until stronger identity evidence is available.

## Follow-up opportunities

- Extend descriptors to other explicitly selected named models after reviewing their references and sources. Do not infer blanket enrichment of generic armament or calibre topics from this pass.
- Review model-level identity before merging related missile families, variants or installation-specific gun topics. Shared words alone do not establish equivalence.
- Check summary presentation on both exact and partial topic searches after future search-data changes; topics without a summary should remain free of Pagefind excerpt text.
- Keep the final validation record honest about which source, generated-data, browser and production checks were actually run.
