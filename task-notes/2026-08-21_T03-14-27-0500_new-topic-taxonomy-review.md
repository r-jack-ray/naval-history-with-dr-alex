## Distinct topics retained

- `jute` and `jutes`: the crop or fibre and the Germanic people are separate subjects.
- `british-rail` and `british-railways`: the post-1965 British Rail name and the earlier British Railways name can mark different corporate periods.
- `sino-soviet-border` and `sino-soviet-border-conflict`: the source concerns frontier planning rather than the named conflict.
- `hms-agincourt-1913` and `hms-agincourt`: the year-qualified topic identifies the actual 1913 battleship; the bare topic's current sources include hypothetical later designs. The qualified display is `HMS Agincourt (1913)`.
- `shipboard-small-arms`, `small-arms`, and `personal-weapons`: the first is a naval-context subset, the second is the general weapon category, and the third is used as a fictional cross-universe category.
- `mark-11-depth-charge`: retain the Arabic-number designation used by current UK official material.
