All 132 literal occurrences of the target phrases, "the lecture" and "this lecture", were found by the checker. They occupied 102 evidence notes, 22 bodies and eight summaries. This coverage check measures the two exact phrases; it does not establish detection of every possible awkward reference to a lecture.

Six agents reviewed disjoint groups using matched fields, nearby segment context and relevant canonical TXT intervals. Three independent second reviews covered every proposed change and retained case. The coordinator reviewed all pairs, integrated ten refinements from those reviews and corrected the same awkward surgical-backlog phrase in an adjacent body. Coverage was limited to those wording decisions and supporting passages.

The 121 changed fields comprise 100 evidence notes, 14 bodies and 7 summaries. Twelve matched fields stayed as written. Natural retained references establish recording time, distinguish estimates or sources, qualify interpretations, or identify photographs discussed in the video. The revised Soviet construction-date body also retains a lecture reference to distinguish its dates from the quoted source.

The pilot supports keeping the rule as a contextual review prompt. It locates substantial repetitive narration, while the retained references show why each sentence needs judgment. Review also caught potential losses of source limitations and planned-versus-existing status before the edits were applied.

For example, "The lecture distinguishes Swift's intended 36 knots from the 34 knots achieved" became "Swift was intended to reach 36 knots but achieved 34 knots." A natural phrase such as "At the time of the lecture" remained where it dates changing programme information.

The canonical wording checker was invoked with every selected path and `--strict --review --summary-only --report`, before and after the changes. Both runs passed. The final scan contains 1,747 other review candidates outside this lecture-reference pilot, with no newly introduced findings from other rules. Byte-level verification confirmed that restoring the 121 approved field edits reproduces each original shard exactly. The other 150 selected shards are byte-identical, and segment structures, identifiers, topics, timestamps and evidence ranges are preserved.
