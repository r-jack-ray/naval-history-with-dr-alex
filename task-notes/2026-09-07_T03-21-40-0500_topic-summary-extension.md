## Independent wording review

An independent reviewer first checked the established summary style and the complete proposed set. The useful pattern was an identifying role followed by one or two stable distinctions, normally within 8 to 16 words. The reviewer rejected title restatements, broad praise or criticism, present-tense status claims, and cloned wording across unresolved topic identities.

The reviewer requested substantive corrections across 18 of the 60 proposed summaries. These changes replaced vague phrases with specific functions or configurations, corrected potentially misleading descriptions, and removed time-sensitive or interpretive claims. Examples include naming the Mark 14 depth-control and exploder defects, identifying SPY-1 as phased-array, describing Yak-38 as a strike aircraft, and stating the defining weapon arrangement of Sverdlov-class cruisers.

After those corrections, the reviewer approved all 60 summaries. The final summaries contain 8 to 15 whitespace-delimited words, none starts with an article, and none duplicates another registry summary. The parallel Freedom and Independence class entries were retained because their matching structure directly contrasts the monohull and trimaran variants.
